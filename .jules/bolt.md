## 2024-10-27 - Composite Indexes for Multi-Tenant Queries
**Learning:** In heavily queried multi-tenant tables, single-column indexes (like `ts` or `status`) are insufficient. Queries filtering by `tenant` and `project_id` result in full table scans unless composite indexes (e.g., `(tenant, project_id, status)`) are added. However, adding these indexes to legacy schemas dynamically requires checking `_column_exists` for all involved columns to avoid `sqlite3.OperationalError` during tests that simulate legacy schemas.
**Action:** When adding composite indexes to multi-tenant tables for performance optimization, always wrap the `CREATE INDEX` statements in `self._column_exists()` checks for each column in the index to ensure backward compatibility and prevent test failures.
