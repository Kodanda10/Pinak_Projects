## 2025-03-01 - Optimizing Vector Store Addition
**Learning:** The O(N) `np.vstack` in `VectorStore.add_vectors` became a significant bottleneck when appending individual vectors repeatedly. The existing threading lock is crucial for data consistency but also prevents concurrency, exacerbating the impact of the O(N) append.
**Action:** Implemented a list-based O(1) buffer (`_vector_buffer`, `_id_buffer`, `_norm_buffer`) for insertions. Appending arrays to Python lists is amortization O(1). The buffers are then flushed into the main numpy arrays (`np.vstack`, `np.concatenate`) in bulk *only* right before operations that require the full array (like `search`, `save`, `reconstruct`, `remove_ids`, etc.). This significantly amortizes the cost of the `vstack`/`concatenate` operations and keeps lock acquisition times much shorter during rapid insertions.
