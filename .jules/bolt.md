## 2024-05-24 - [Composite Indexes for Multi-Tenant Tables]
**Learning:** In heavily queried multi-tenant tables, single-column indexes on timestamp or status fields are often insufficient because queries usually filter by `tenant` and `project_id` first. This can lead to N+1 queries or full table scans for a specific tenant/project. Benchmarking reveals `logs_client_issues` doesn't benefit from this, but `logs_access`, `memory_quarantine`, `clients_registry`, `logs_agents` do.
**Action:** When optimizing multi-tenant databases, append composite database indexes covering the multi-tenant isolation columns `(tenant, project_id)` alongside the timestamp or status fields. Also ensure all columns exist before creating indexes in `.execute` logic to prevent errors in legacy schema simulation tests.
