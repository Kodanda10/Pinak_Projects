## 2025-03-01 - Add Composite Indexes on Multi-tenant Tables
**Learning:** In the `Pinak_Services/memory_service` SQLite database schema, heavily queried multi-tenant tables like `logs_access`, `memory_quarantine`, `clients_registry`, `logs_agents`, and `logs_client_issues` only had single-column indexes on timestamps or status flags. Because queries invariably filter by `tenant` and `project_id`, the lack of composite indexes including these multi-tenant columns can lead to suboptimal query execution or full table scans, negating the benefit of the single-column indexes for typical workloads.
**Action:** Added composite indexes across `tenant`, `project_id`, and the commonly queried secondary columns (like `status` or `last_seen DESC`). Avoided replacing the single-column indexes outright, as they may still be useful, and ensured composite indexes cover the primary multi-tenant filtering axes to optimize standard read queries.
